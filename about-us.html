About Us

Blueworld Cosmetics Limited is the leading cosmetics product manufacturer in Nigeria, with well-known brands produced and distributed across the country and the West African Sub Region, leveraging majorly on locally sourced materials and a well-motivated technical and managerial workforce bonded by very strong core values. Its range of products cut-across skin care, body care, haircare, soap, body bath, and perfumes. We have so far lived up to our mission and strategic goal of producing and marketing quality, safe and affordable cosmetics products in Nigeria. With Head Office and production facility in Lagos, and regional offices in Abuja, Akwa, Ibadan, Benin, Uyo, Nsukka, and Kano, the company currently employs over 400 workers of different cadre, thus directly contributing to domestic household purchasing power and the nations gross domestic product (GDP). We have continued to grow in asset base and production capability in response to an ever growing demand for our quality and market leading products. We are a member of the Manufacturers Association of Nigeria and our products are registered with regulatory authorities, especially NAFDAC and SON.
