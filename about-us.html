About Us

Blue World Cosmetics is a leading cosmetics company dedicated to providing high-quality beauty products that enhance the natural beauty of our customers. With a commitment to innovation, sustainability, and customer satisfaction, we strive to create products that empower individuals to feel confident and beautiful in their own skin.

Office Locations

With Head Office and production facility in Lagos, and regional offices in Abuja, Akwa, Ibadan, Benin, Uyo, Nsukka, and Kano, the company currently employs over 400 workers of different cadre, thus directly contributing to domestic household purchasing power and the nations gross domestic product (GDP).
